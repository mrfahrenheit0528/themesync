
\chapter{Results and Discussion}
\begin{refsection}
This chapter presents the results of the evaluation and testing conducted on ThemeSync, the proposed graph-based design orchestration framework. It highlights the performance of the Graph Neural Network, the Cassowary constraint solver, and the overall reliability of the system in synchronizing brand assets across heterogeneous design formats. The results are discussed in relation to the objectives of the study, identifying the framework's strengths and limitations for real-world deployment.
